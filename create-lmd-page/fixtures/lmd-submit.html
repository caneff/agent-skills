Create a New Puzzle

To create a new puzzle please fill in the following form. After saving the puzzle you can preview and correct it without other users being notified. To make the puzzle available to the puzzle solver community you have to choose Activate on the puzzle page.

If this is the first time that you try to publish a puzzle we recommend that you read the hints section on the instructions page. This will help you avoid frequent beginner's mistakes.

Please choose significant labels and tags (see below) for your puzzle. They make it easier for the other users of the portal to find the puzzle types they are looking for. The labels and tags are supposed to designate puzzle types or variants, not just repeat keywords from the puzzle title.
